Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 22, 2026

Welcome to MiniGames! This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a safe gaming experience.

1. Information We Collect

MiniGames is designed with your privacy in mind. While we focus on a minimal data approach, please note the following:

  • No Personal Identity: We do not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • Local Storage: Game scores and progress are stored locally on your device. This data stays on your phone.
  • Device Identifiers: Our advertising partners may collect non-personal information such as your Advertising ID to serve relevant ads.

2. How We Use Information

The information processed is used to:

  • Maintain your game progress and high scores.
  • Display advertisements within the app.
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs.

3. Third-Party Services (Advertising)

MiniGames uses third-party advertising services to support the development of the app. These services may collect and use non-personal data (like your device's advertising ID) to show you relevant ads:

  • Google AdMob: We use AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more details on how they handle data.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information processed by the app. Since most game data is stored locally, your device's security settings (like screen locks) are the primary protection for your scores.

5. Children's Privacy

MiniGames is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

6. Permissions

MiniGames requires the following permissions:

  • Internet Access: Required to load advertisements and app assets.
  • Network State: To check if a connection is available for showing ads.

7. Data Deletion

You can reset your game data by clearing the app's cache/data in your Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Since we don't store your data on our own servers, uninstalling the app removes all local progress.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

9. Consent

By using MiniGames, you consent to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.
